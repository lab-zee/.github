# Lab Z community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, safe, and inclusive community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, appearance, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact.
- Welcome questions and differing technical perspectives.
- Give and receive feedback without personal attacks.
- Protect private information and respect consent and boundaries.
- Help create an environment where people can participate without harassment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, stalking, doxxing,
deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publishing private
information without permission are not acceptable. Abusive behavior in public
or private spaces connected to a Lab Z project may be considered.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not litigate sensitive conduct reports in public issues. Report concerns
privately through an established organization maintainer channel or GitHub's
applicable abuse-reporting tools. Share only the information needed to assess
the concern.

Maintainers will review reports as confidentially and promptly as practical,
avoid conflicts of interest, and may remove content, issue a warning, restrict
participation, or permanently ban a participant. Retaliation against someone
who raises a good-faith concern is prohibited.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying a private reporting path in
repository-specific documentation when the default organization channels are
insufficient.
